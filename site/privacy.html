Yesterdo

Privacy

Yesterdo does not collect any data. Last updated 11 October 2026.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, settings and the pictures you add are stored on your device. The developer has no server that receives them and no way to see them.

iCloud

If your device is signed into iCloud, your tasks and their pictures sync between your own devices through Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. That data is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to the developer. Settings are never synced. Signed out, nothing leaves the device.

Reminders and photos

Reminders are scheduled on the device as local notifications. Pictures are taken with the camera or chosen through the system photo picker, only when you add one to a task; only those pictures are kept in the app.

No tracking

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs that collect data, and no tracking across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Delete a task in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. Synced data can be removed in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Children

Yesterdo collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
