Pick a look as you scroll:

One day at a time

Today, and nothing more.

Each day holds its own list. Swipe to tomorrow to plan ahead, or back to see what you did. Today stays short enough to finish.

  • Pin what matters to the top of its day.
  • Times and reminders that call when a task is due.
  • Repeats every day, on chosen weekdays, or monthly.

Nothing piles up

Unfinished? That's fine.

Yesterday's leftovers wait quietly under Left behind. Bring one to today, send it to another day, or let it go. No red badges, no guilt.

  • Carry over moves a task to today on its own.
  • Ignore if not done lets a task pass without a word.
  • Snooze or Not today when a reminder comes at the wrong time.

More than a line

Checklists, notes and pictures.

A task can hold a whole plan. Tick items off as you go and they sink out of the way. Add a picture from the camera or your photos.

  • Paste a bulleted list and it comes in as a checklist.
  • Share a task's words with any app.
  • Search across every day you've written.

Four looks

Yours, in light and dark.

Ink, Ocean, Forest and Blossom, each with its own app icon. Tap one to see this page in it.

Everywhere you are

iPhone and iPad, in sync.

Your tasks follow you through your own iCloud. Widgets for the Home Screen show what's due and what's pinned.
