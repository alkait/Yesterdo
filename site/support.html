Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to alkait+yesterdo@gmail.com. I read every message.

Tasks don't sync between devices

Make sure each device is signed into the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive is on. Sync happens when the app is opened or brought to the front.

Reminders don't arrive

Check iOS Settings › Notifications › Yesterdo, and that Focus isn't silencing it.

A task from yesterday disappeared

Unfinished tasks wait under Left behind. Turn on Carry over in a task to bring it to today automatically.

Including details

When reporting a bug, add your device, iOS version and the app version shown in Settings.
